## Rule and project policy

The [official FAQ](https://itch.io/jam/thatgamejam02/topic/7110933/thatgamejam02-faq) requires clear explanation of AI use at submission. The [compliance review](01-competition-compliance.md) records the rule and its limits. This project's conservative disclosure includes planning/documentation assistance even if no generated art is shipped. It is not a claim that organizer-reviewed AI clearance has occurred.

AI may assist post-start coding, wording, debugging, or art only within phase scope and independently reviewed rights. No generator is approved for copied franchise likenesses or style-matched game-ready replicas. Technical suggestions require actual testing. Generated content receives the same asset/provenance scrutiny as external assets; no default assumption of exclusive rights or license safety.

## Ongoing log procedure

For each future use, append date, tool/model as actually available, purpose, affected source/asset paths, what was generated versus edited, author/reviewer, retained evidence, license/provenance issues, and whether the result ships. Record rejected material when needed to explain removal. Do not log credentials or sensitive prompts. Keep code assistance distinct from generated art/audio/text delivered to players.
