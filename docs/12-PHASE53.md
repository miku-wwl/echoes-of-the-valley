## Gate C: pond and original wooden structure

The old pond disc and six green cylinder renderers remain in the copied scene for comparison, disabled in Phase53. A gridded 3D
shore/water surface uses the cleaned S0/S3 paintings, a 14-point irregular inner contour, a separate carved TerrainData with shallow
edges descending to a real basin floor, and a solid static deep-water exclusion mesh. Six alpha-cut lily clusters replace the six
plain cylinders. A source-image gap preserves the existing incoming stream. S0/S3 paintings switch conservatively, with stage-based
colour grading for S1/S2, avoiding crossfade ghosts. The existing ResonanceStage remains the only progression authority.

The original wooden structure is actually **Dock**, nine planks projecting from the east bank, not a bridge spanning both shores.
Its transforms, planks and appearance are preserved. The formerly visual-only planks now have collision; water boundaries protect
the sides and the closed end. Acceptance checks walk to its end, turn and return, and attempt water entry from three other banks.
No new crossing or new region is invented. If a two-bank bridge is required, that remains outside this preserved-layout implementation.

The water tiling candidate is seam-corrected (edge RGB error 15.37/11.91 becomes 0/0) but retained as an optional production/reference
texture, not misrepresented as the opaque painted pond's moving water. The pond has a real world surface and carved depth, not a
camera-facing floating pond card. The minimap is re-rendered from the changed valley with the original bounds and live marker code.
