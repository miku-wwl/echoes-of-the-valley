What the scripts do to the pixels, and nothing else: cut the figures from the parchment (alpha from colour distance and GrabCut; parchment
showing between hair locks made transparent; edge colours taken from the nearest solid pixel so no light halo remains), warp them with
2D bones (linear-blend skinning, cubic resampling), composite layers, downscale (area filter), mirror the right-facing view, remove
three left-side accessories from that view, and fill the few pixels that become visible behind removed or moved parts by OpenCV
inpainting from neighbouring pixels of the same illustration. **No pixel is drawn, painted or generated by code, and no other image
(game, photo, stock, marketplace, Atelier/Koei Tecmo material) is used.**
