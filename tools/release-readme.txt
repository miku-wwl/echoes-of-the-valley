ECHOES OF THE VALLEY
A short, peaceful adventure about bringing a silent valley back to life.
Made for thatgamejam #02 (2026).   Release candidate 1, Windows 64-bit.

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Unzip the whole folder anywhere (do not run the game from inside the zip).
  2. Run EchoesOfTheValley.exe. There is nothing to install and no internet connection is needed.
  3. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card that supports DirectX 11.

  Gather herbs, blossoms and ore, feed Clover the hen, mix potions at the alchemy
  workshop and help Mira, Wren and Alder restore the stream, the garden and the
  windmill. The valley's colours, light, water, life and music change with each
  restoration. A full play takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

CONTROLS
  WASD or arrow keys   Move (relative to the camera)
  Mouse                Look around
  E                    Talk / gather / use (also advances dialogue)
  I                    Open or close the satchel
  1, 2, 3              Craft the 1st, 2nd or 3rd recipe at the alchemy workshop
  Esc                  Close a panel, or pause (Resume, Save Game, Main Menu, Quit)
  On the ending screen: Enter, E or Esc continue exploring; M returns to the title screen.
  Music and sound volume sliders are on the title screen and in the pause menu.

SAVING
  The game saves at every story step and from the pause menu (Save Game).
  Continue on the title screen loads the save. New Game asks before replacing a save.
  Saves and settings live in:
    %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\EchoesJam\Echoes of the Valley\
  Delete echoes_save.json there to start completely fresh.
